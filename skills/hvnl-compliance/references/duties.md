# HVNL Duties — Who Gets Fucked and How to Pre-empt It

| Duty | Section | Who it bites | Pre-empt in app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unfit to drive | New statutory duty (Amendment Act) | Driver must not drive if unfit. Operator must not cause or permit. | Fit-for-duty hard-block + timestamped declaration. False declaration = offence for both. |
| Fatigue / work hours | Work diary + hours rules | Driver and operator | Hours available gate before start. Rest taken confirmation. |
| Chain of Responsibility | Multiple | Every party in chain (driver, operator, scheduler, loader) | Checklist that forces evidence at each hand-off. |
| Executive due diligence | s26D | Company executives / operators | Second layer for business side if not pure owner-driver. |
| Mass and dimension | Mass regs | Driver, operator, loader | Verification step before dispatch with photo/evidence. |
| Vehicle standards | Various | Driver, operator | Pre-start checklist that sucks failures into black hole (fail = remove). |

Use these citations on every screen. Do not paraphrase the offence — quote the consequence.
